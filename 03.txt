# Stories from Our Neighbourhood
## Past events and community memories

This lesson practises narrative order and descriptive detail.

### Step 1. A surprising morning

@media video video/morning-story.webm
@script A surprising morning {
Narrator: I was walking to work when I heard music in the square.
Narrator: A brass band was playing, and everyone was dancing.
Narrator: I stopped for five minutes, but I stayed for an hour.
}

@task gist
@instr Choose the best summary.
@choice Choose one answer. {
The narrator missed work because the bus broke down.
The narrator unexpectedly joined a community celebration.
The narrator practised music alone before work.
}

@task detail-check
@instr Answer each question by choosing Yes or No.
@matching Answer the questions. {
Was the narrator walking to work?
Was the square silent?
Did the narrator leave after five minutes?
---
Yes
No
}

@task story-order
@instr Put the events in the order in which they happened.
@ordering Put the events in order. {
The narrator heard music.
The narrator saw people dancing.
The narrator decided to stop.
The narrator stayed for an hour.
}

@header Past continuous and past simple
@example Contrast {
I was walking to work
---
when I heard music.
}
@wordlist {
walk — walked — walked
hear — heard — heard
see — saw — seen
stay — stayed — stayed
}

@task grammar-story
@instr Complete the story with suitable verb forms.
@multifill Complete the sentences. {
I _____ (walk) home when I _____ (see) my neighbour.
She _____ (carry) a large cake, so I _____ (offer) to help.
}
@key Answers {
was walking; saw
was carrying; offered
}

### Step 2. Collect a local memory

@task interview
@editor Confirm that learners are comfortable sharing personal memories.
@speaking
@instr Interview a partner about a memorable local event.
@question Where did the event happen?
@question What were you doing when it began?
@question Why do you remember it?
@multifill {
Place: _____
People: _____
Main event: _____
}

@step Step 1. Listen
@note Listen without interrupting.

@step Step 2. Clarify
@instr Ask at least two follow-up questions.

@step Step 3. Retell
@alt Retell an event from a book or film if you prefer not to share a personal memory.
@multifill Plan your retelling. {
At first, _____
While _____, _____
In the end, _____
}
