# Practical English: A Weekend in York
## Travel, directions, and polite requests

### Step 1. Plan the journey

In this lesson, you will plan a city break, ask for directions, and make polite requests.

You will also practise language for railway stations and hotels.

@media image "media/york city map.jpg"
@note The map shows the station, the city walls, and the main sights.

@header Essential travel words

@wordlist station; platform; return ticket; city walls; guest house
@wordlist Useful phrases {
a single ticket
a return ticket
the next train
on the left
straight ahead
}

@example The next train leaves at half past nine.
@example Question and answer {
How can I get to the city walls?
---
Go straight ahead and turn left at the museum.
}
@example Natural alternatives {
Could I have a return ticket to York, please?
Can I have a return ticket to York, please?
}

### Step 2. Find the hotel

@task directions
@speaking
@media image maps/hotel-route.png
@instr Work with a partner. Ask for and give directions.
@choice Choose the best opening question. {
Where hotel?
Excuse me, how can I get to the hotel?
You show hotel.
}
@question Which landmark do you pass after the museum?
@alt A learner working alone can record both parts of the dialogue.

@step Stage 1. Prepare
@multichoice Select all the useful direction phrases. {
Go straight ahead.
Turn right at the bank.
I travelled yesterday.
It is opposite the theatre.
}

@step Stage 2. Speak
@script Model dialogue {
Visitor: Excuse me, how can I get to the Minster Guest House?
Local: Go straight ahead, then turn right at the museum.
Visitor: Is it far?
Local: No, it is opposite the theatre.
}

@step Stage 3. Reflect
@multifill Write two sentences about your route. {
First, I _____

Then, I _____
}
@endtask

@task check-in-order
@instr Put the stages of check-in in a logical order.
@ordering {
Greet the receptionist.
Give your name.
Show your identification.
Collect the room key.
}
@key {
1. Greet the receptionist.
2. Give your name.
3. Show your identification.
4. Collect the room key.
}

@task hotel-details
@instr Match each question to the available types of answer.

}

@note {
Use “What time …?” for a time and “Where …?” for a place.
